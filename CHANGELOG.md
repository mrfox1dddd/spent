# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Time per app: day, week, month, year, all time
- 24-hour dial for today
- Notification counter per app (counted from the moment access is granted)
- Per-app details: time, launches, notifications
- Export of the full history to CSV and JSON
- Light and dark themes, three accent colors
- English and Russian
- No internet permission, no cloud backup
